PRIVACY POLICY

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We do not require user registration or accounts. When you purchase a subscription, Google Play processes your payment. We only receive basic, non-personally identifiable transaction data. This includes:

  • Order ID and purchase time.
  • An anonymous subscription purchase token.
  • Country-level location data used strictly for tax and pricing purposes.

2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

Because we do not use accounts, analytics trackers, or custom databases, we have no access to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your credit/debit card numbers or billing details.
  • Your precise location or device identity logs.
  • Any data from children, as our app is not directed toward users under 13.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the basic transaction metadata solely to:

  • Activate your premium features on your device.
  • Verify active renewals or subscription cancellations.
  • Resolve payment troubleshooting requests.

4. THIRD-PARTY BILLING SERVICES

All billing operations are securely handled by Google Play Services. Their collection and use of your payment information are governed directly by the Google Privacy Policy.
